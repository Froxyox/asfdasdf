How the sliders work

The three weights always sum to 100%. Moving one slider redistributes the change equally across the other two — so if you raise Reputation by 10, Search and Site each drop by 5. Use the reset button to return to the default 50 / 30 / 20 split.

What each dimension measures

Reputation
Google star rating (floor at 3.0), review count (log-scaled so large chains don't dominate), and what % of reviews are recent (last 12 months).
Search Visibility
Whether the business appears in Google's local 3-pack and its average keyword rank for relevant search terms — both measured from the same city-center location for consistency.
Site Quality
Google PageSpeed performance score, a full site audit (broken links, missing tags, duplicate content), and mobile usability.
