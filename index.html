Process

Three signals. One score.

Each business is evaluated across three weighted dimensions, combined into a single 0–100 ranking number.

1

Pull business data

We collect Google ratings, review counts and recency, local pack positions, keyword ranks, and site audit scores for every business in the category.

2

Normalize each signal

Review counts are log-scaled so large chains don't dominate. Ratings use a floor at 3.0. Pack positions map to a 0–100 curve. Every metric lands on the same scale.

3

Apply weighted formula

Reputation 50% + Search Visibility 30% + Site Quality 20% = final score. Weights are adjustable — tune them live on the leaderboard page.

4

Filter low-data businesses

Businesses with fewer than 10 reviews are marked "unranked" rather than given a misleading score. The minimum is adjustable.
